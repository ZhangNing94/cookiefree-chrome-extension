Privacy Policy - CookieFree

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

Data Collection

CookieFree is a local privacy cleaner. All cleaning operations happen locally in your browser. The extension reads cookie data to display and manage it, but no data is ever sent to external servers.

Permissions

CookieFree requires the "browsingData" permission to clear browsing data (cookies, cache, history, etc.). The "cookies" permission is used to list, view, and delete cookies. The "alarms" permission enables scheduled auto-cleaning. No data leaves your browser.

Data Storage

Your whitelist and auto-clean settings are stored locally using Chrome's storage API. This data is never transmitted externally.

Important Note

Clearing cookies will log you out of websites. Use the whitelist feature to protect cookies for sites you want to stay logged in to. Clearing LocalStorage may reset website settings and data.
